# IT Interview Guides

**Practical interview preparation for IT careers — from beginner to professional.**

IT Interview Guides is a free educational initiative by **IT Education Nepal (ITEN)** designed to help students, freshers, career changers, and IT professionals prepare for real-world technical interviews.

The project provides structured, role-based interview preparation covering technical fundamentals, troubleshooting, practical scenarios, follow-up questions, communication skills, interview strategy, and career-focused revision.

Our goal is not simply to help learners memorize interview answers. We want learners to **understand the technology, explain it clearly, troubleshoot logically, and build the confidence required for real IT roles.**

---

## 🎯 Project Mission

Many learners understand technical concepts but have limited opportunities to practice how those concepts are applied in real jobs and technical interviews.

IT Interview Guides aims to help bridge the gap between:

**Theory → Practical Skills → Interview Readiness → Career**

The guides are designed to be useful across different countries and employment environments while remaining vendor-neutral wherever possible.

Employer-specific procedures, permissions, escalation paths, technologies, and policies may differ.

---

## 👥 Who These Guides Are For

This project is designed for:

- University and college students
- Fresh graduates and freshers
- Entry-level IT professionals
- Career changers entering technology
- Technical certification learners
- L1 and L2 support professionals
- Experienced professionals preparing for new roles
- Trainers, mentors, and educators

Some guides focus on beginner and entry-level positions, while others will progressively cover intermediate and advanced IT roles.

## 🧠 How the Guides Are Designed

Depending on the role, guides may include:

- Interview questions and structured answers
- Follow-up questions
- Troubleshooting scenarios
- Technical fundamentals
- Real-world support situations
- Diagnostic tools and commands
- Communication questions
- Behavioral interview preparation
- Security and operational best practices
- Job-description analysis
- Mock interviews
- Rapid-fire revision
- Career preparation
- Study plans

The focus is always on understanding and practical reasoning rather than memorizing scripted answers.

## ⚠️ Important Note

These guides provide educational interview preparation and examples of generally accepted IT practices.

Actual responsibilities, permissions, tools, escalation procedures, security policies, priority definitions, and operating procedures vary between organizations.

Candidates should describe their real experience accurately. When discussing something they have not personally performed, they should explain what they **would do** rather than presenting hypothetical experience as actual work experience.
